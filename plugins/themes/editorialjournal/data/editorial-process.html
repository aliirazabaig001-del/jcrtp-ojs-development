Manuscripts submitted to JCRTP move through the eight stages below. Peer review informs the editor's judgement, and the editor communicates each decision to the corresponding author. Handling time depends on the manuscript; no fixed turnaround is stated.

Editorial workflow

  1. Online manuscript submission

    Authors submit through the journal's online system, providing the manuscript files, author information, declarations and metadata it requests. Journal-specific requirements are given in the author guidelines.

  2. Initial editorial screening

    The editorial office checks that the submission is complete, and the editor considers whether it is suitable for further assessment. A submission may be returned for missing information or may not proceed.

  3. Handling editor assessment

    An editor assesses the manuscript's relevance, contribution and readiness, and may seek external reviews or make an editorial decision.

  4. Independent peer review

    When external review is requested, independent reviewers provide expert assessments to inform the editor.

  5. Editorial decision

    The editor considers the submission and any reviews, then communicates the decision and any guidance to the corresponding author.

  6. Manuscript revision

    When revision is invited, authors respond to the editor's and reviewers' comments and submit revised files through the system, as set out in the decision letter.

  7. Final acceptance

    Acceptance is an editorial decision communicated by the journal. Submission, peer review or a request to revise does not guarantee publication.

  8. Production and online publication

    Accepted manuscripts are prepared for publication. The journal explains any action needed from the authors, and the article is published online once production is complete.

Editorial decisions

Accept
The manuscript is accepted and proceeds to production.
Minor revision
Revisions are requested before the editor completes consideration.
Major revision
Substantial revisions are requested before further consideration.
Reject
The manuscript is declined for publication in the journal.

The decision letter is authoritative for each submission. A request to revise does not guarantee subsequent acceptance.

Questions about a decision

Authors with a concern about an editorial decision or process should contact the editorial office, identifying the manuscript and the matter they wish to raise.
