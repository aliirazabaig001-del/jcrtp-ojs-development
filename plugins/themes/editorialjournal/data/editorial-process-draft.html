At a glance

Eight stages, one considered process

Open any stage for practical guidance. Individual handling and timing depend on the manuscript and editorial correspondence; no fixed turnaround is stated here.

  1. 01Online Manuscript Submission

    Authors submit through the journal's online submission workflow and provide the files, author information, declarations, and manuscript metadata requested by the system. Consult the current author instructions for journal-specific requirements.

  2. 02Initial Editorial Screening

    The editorial office checks submission completeness and the editor considers whether the manuscript is suitable for further assessment. A submission may be returned for missing information or may not proceed. Screening criteria are outlined below.

  3. 03Handling Editor Assessment

    An editor assesses the manuscript's relevance, contribution, and readiness for the next step. The editor may seek external reviews or make an editorial decision in accordance with the journal's approved policies.

  4. 04Independent Peer Review

    When external review is requested, independent reviewers provide expert assessments to inform the editor. Reviewer identity arrangements, reviewer numbers, and confidentiality procedures must follow the journal's confirmed review policy; no single- or double-anonymous model is assumed here.

  5. 05Editorial Decision

    The editor considers the submission and any available reviews, then communicates a decision and any relevant guidance to the corresponding author. Possible decision categories are shown in the decision pathway.

  6. 06Manuscript Revision

    When revision is invited, authors should respond to the editor's and reviewers' comments and submit revised files through the system. The decision letter specifies the requested changes and any applicable response date.

  7. 07Final Acceptance

    Acceptance is an editorial decision communicated by the journal. A revision request is not an acceptance, and submission or peer review does not guarantee publication.

  8. 08Production and Online Publication

    Accepted manuscripts proceed through preparation for publication. Production correspondence will explain any author action required; the article is made available online after the journal completes its production and release steps.

How decisions work

Editorial decision pathway

Peer review informs editorial judgment; the editor communicates the decision. Review is sought when selected as appropriate for the submission.

  1. Manuscript submitted
  2. Initial editorial screening
  3. Handling editor assessment
  4. External peer review, if selected
  5. Editorial decision

Possible decision categories

  • AcceptProceed to acceptance and production.
  • Minor RevisionAddress the editor's specified revisions for further consideration.
  • Major RevisionAddress substantial requested revisions for further consideration.
  • RejectThe manuscript will not proceed in its current submission.

↶ A revised manuscript may return to editorial assessment and, where the editor considers it appropriate, further peer review. A revision decision is not a promise of acceptance.

Editorial oversight

Editorial screening criteria

At screening, editors may consider the following general aspects when deciding whether a submission is ready and suitable for further consideration.

  • Relevance to the journal's scope and readership.
  • Submission completeness and clarity of presentation.
  • Whether the research question and contribution are understandable.
  • Availability of information needed for editorial and scientific assessment.
  • Ethics statements and disclosures relevant to the work, where applicable.

These are explanatory considerations, not a complete or ranked acceptance checklist. The editor may request missing information or decide that a submission will not proceed.

Independent expertise

Reviewer selection and confidentiality

When external peer review is selected, the handling editor seeks independent assessments relevant to the manuscript. The editor considers the reviews as part of the decision process and remains responsible for the editorial decision.

Reviewers and editors should treat unpublished submission materials with appropriate care. The precise confidentiality, competing-interest, reviewer-identity, and review-communication rules must be confirmed in JCRTP's approved policy before publication of this page.

Editorial confirmation required: Review model (including any anonymous-review arrangements), reviewer eligibility and selection, number of reviews, conflicts of interest, and permitted disclosure of review reports.

Assessment

Peer review and scientific evaluation

Reviewers may provide a reasoned assessment to help the editor evaluate a manuscript. Depending on the work, relevant considerations can include:

  • Importance and relevance of the research question.
  • Clarity of the methods and whether conclusions are supported by the presented evidence.
  • Interpretation of results, limitations, and relevance to the field.
  • Clarity, completeness, and organization of the manuscript.
  • Ethical and reporting information relevant to the study.

These considerations support editorial assessment; they do not establish a score, threshold, or guaranteed outcome.

The editor's response

Editorial decision categories

01

Accept

The editor accepts the manuscript and it can proceed to production.

02

Minor Revision

Revisions are requested before the editor completes consideration.

03

Major Revision

Substantial revisions are requested before further consideration.

04

Reject

The editor declines the manuscript for publication in the journal.

The decision letter is authoritative for the submission. A request to revise does not guarantee subsequent acceptance.

Responding to a decision

Revised manuscript requirements

  1. Use the decision letter and reviewer comments to identify the requested changes.
  2. Submit a revised manuscript and a clear response to the comments through the journal's online system, following the instructions in the decision letter.
  3. Indicate where changes were made and explain respectfully where a suggestion was not adopted.
  4. Check that revised files and submission metadata are complete before resubmission.

Any response date is provided in the editorial correspondence. This page does not set a universal revision deadline or promise an outcome.

Responsible scholarship

Research ethics and publication integrity

Authors are responsible for providing accurate information about their work and for including ethics review, consent, registration, funding, and competing-interest disclosures when relevant and requested by the journal.

Editors may seek clarification when information needed to assess a submission is absent or unclear. Concerns about research or publication integrity should be raised with the editorial office for appropriate consideration under the journal's approved policies.

To confirm: JCRTP-specific ethics requirements, investigation procedures, correction and retraction policy, and any adopted external standards should be linked from the journal's approved publication-ethics policy.

After the decision

Acceptance and production

After an acceptance decision, the manuscript proceeds to production checks and preparation for publication. The journal may contact authors to resolve production queries or confirm publication details. The journal's correspondence will identify any action needed from the authors.

Acceptance and online release are distinct steps. Article publication follows completion of production and the journal's release process; no publication date or production timetable is promised on this page.

To confirm: Any applicable publication charges, licensing choices, production requirements, and expected publication sequence must match the journal's approved and current author information.

Author review

Proof corrections

When proofs are sent, authors should review the material carefully and return corrections using the instructions and response date provided with the proof request. Raise questions about substantive changes with the editorial or production contact before making them.

Proof-return deadlines and the scope of permitted changes are set by the production communication; no universal deadline is stated here.

The published record

Online publication

Following production and journal release, the final article is made available through JCRTP. The published version and its article page provide the authoritative record and any available article-level publication information.

Questions or concerns

Editorial appeals and complaints

Authors with a concern about an editorial decision or process should contact the journal and clearly identify the manuscript and the matter they wish to raise. Use the journal contact page to reach the editorial office.

Editorial confirmation required before publication: The appeal grounds, submission method, time limit, review authority, complaint escalation route, and expected handling process are not provided in the available source material.

Frequently asked questions

Editorial process FAQs

How long does review take?

No fixed review timeframe is stated here. The time needed varies with the manuscript and editorial process. Any status update should be requested through the journal's established contact route.

Does peer review guarantee acceptance?

No. Peer review informs editorial consideration; submission, review, and revision do not guarantee acceptance or publication.

What should I include with a revision?

Follow the decision letter. In general, provide the revised manuscript and a clear response to the comments, together with any files requested by the editor.

Which peer-review model does JCRTP use?

The review model was not specified in the available source material. The journal should confirm and publish its reviewer-identity and confidentiality arrangements.

Are there publication charges?

This page does not state or imply a charge. Consult the journal's current, approved fee information or contact the editorial office.
